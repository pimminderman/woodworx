Project 07 / Understairs storage

Space beneath the stairs

Panelled storage that follows the staircase, making use of an awkward space.

Type

Fitted understairs cabinetry

Details

Stepped proportions · panelled doors

Finish

Light painted joinery

Every angle has its purpose.

The slope beneath a staircase is a natural opportunity for fitted storage. Here, a series of panelled doors follows the changing height of the stairs.

The painted finish and traditional detailing sit alongside the existing staircase. Instead of leaving the area as an open recess, the cabinetry gives it a neat, enclosed face.

Understairs storage can be designed for different needs, from drawers to shelving and hanging space. The layout depends on the available depth and what needs to be stored.
