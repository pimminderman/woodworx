Project 03 / Window seat & storage

The window, reimagined

A fitted seat that brings storage and a comfortable perch to a bay window.

Type

Built-in bay-window seat

Details

Hinged storage · radiator cover

Finish

Painted joinery

Making room for a quiet moment.

The space beneath a window can do more than frame the view. This fitted bench turns the bay into a place to sit, while making use of the space below.

A hinged top gives access to concealed storage. The existing radiator cover was retained and incorporated into the design, keeping a familiar feature of the room.

The gallery shows the joinery taking shape before the finished seat was dressed with cushions.
