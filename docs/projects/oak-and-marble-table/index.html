Project 02 / Bespoke furniture

Oak meets marble

A dining table designed around a piece of marble the client already owned.

Type

Freestanding dining table

Materials

Oak · client-owned marble

Brief

Designed around an existing top

An existing material. A new purpose.

This commission began with a piece of marble already belonging to the client. The task was to turn it into a dining table that would suit their room.

The slim rectangular form makes considered use of the dining space. Below the marble, an oak base gives the table its distinctive character, with vertical timber detailing creating a contrast to the surface above.

Designed in conversation with the client, the finished piece brings an existing material into everyday use.
