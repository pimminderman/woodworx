Project 05 / Home office

A little space to work

An alcove becomes a dedicated work area, with a fitted desk and shelves above.

Type

Fitted desk & shelving

Details

Alcove desktop · integrated storage

Finish

White painted joinery

A workspace that belongs at home.

A home office does not always need its own room. This desk fits into an existing alcove, using its width to create a defined place to work.

Shelves above keep books and everyday objects close at hand. Storage beneath the desktop helps keep the working surface clear, while the light finish sits quietly within the room.

The result is a compact arrangement in which the desk, shelves and storage are considered together.
