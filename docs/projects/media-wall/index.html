Project 06 / Media & display cabinetry

More than a media wall

A wall of shelving, display space and fitted furniture, composed as one piece.

Type

Fitted media & display unit

Details

Open shelves · central recess · desk

Finish

Light painted cabinetry

Bringing a wall together.

This fitted unit combines open shelving with a central recess and a desk surface below. Different shelf sizes create places for books, display pieces and everyday essentials.

Rather than treating each function separately, the joinery brings them into one continuous arrangement. A light finish keeps the overall composition understated.

The second view offers a closer look at the proportions and the way the lower surface meets the shelving.
