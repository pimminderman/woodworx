Project 04 / Fitted wardrobes

Storage, beautifully fitted

Full-height fitted wardrobes with panelled doors and drawers at the centre.

Type

Fitted bedroom storage

Details

Panelled doors · central drawers

Finish

Grey painted cabinetry

A considered use of the whole wall.

A fitted wardrobe brings storage into the architecture of a bedroom. In this example, a continuous run of painted cabinetry fills the wall, with tall doors on either side of a central drawer section.

The repeated panel detailing gives the larger installation a calm, consistent rhythm. Overhead lighting picks out the soft grey finish.

Wardrobe interiors can be planned around the way you use them: hanging rails, shelves, drawers, or a combination. The arrangement is discussed for each commission.
