Project 08 / Bespoke bathroom furniture

A warmer kind of bathroom

A curved timber vanity brings texture and warmth to a tiled bathroom.

Type

Bespoke vanity cabinet

Details

Curved front · vertical timber slats

Setting

Bathroom

A small piece with a strong presence.

This bathroom vanity pairs a white countertop basin with a timber cabinet below. A curved front softens its outline, while closely spaced vertical slats give the piece texture.

The natural timber tones sit against pale tiling and complement the warm metal taps above. It is a compact example of how a bespoke piece can shape the character of a room.
